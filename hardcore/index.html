Hardcore mode

The daily equation, against the clock. You get one attempt, and the timer starts the moment you begin typing.

  • 60 seconds. When it hits zero, the round is over.
  • 6 guesses, with the same green / yellow / grey feedback.
  • Every wrong calculation counts. A guess that doesn't add up (like 10 × 12 = 119) burns a guess and turns red, with no colour hints.

No replays, so make them count. Good luck.
